## The two questions people ask first

**"What happens if my policy engine goes down?"** Nothing gets signed. Every failure mode — timeout, crash,
malformed JSON, bad MAC, unreachable host — withholds the signature. An outage delays transactions; it can
never authorize one. The one deliberate exception is outbound notifications, which are best-effort by design
and never gate signing. See [INTEGRATION.md](INTEGRATION.md).

**"Who holds the key?"** You do. It is generated by you, held in your process or in your Vault, and cannot
sign without an explicit `approve` from your engine. Certen cannot sign with it.
See [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md).
